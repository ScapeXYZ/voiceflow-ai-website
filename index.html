What is working in the current demonstration?

Sarah handles fictional plumbing inquiries in Retell AI. Structured call summaries are delivered by email through a Cloudflare Worker and Google Apps Script. The video reenacts this workflow; the page does not place a live call.

Can Sarah book appointments or dispatch a plumber?

The current demo captures preferred follow-up windows and marks bookings as requested, not confirmed. A person reviews the request and confirms next steps. Live phone answering, calendar booking, and dispatch require separate integrations.

How are urgent problems handled?

The demonstration identifies reported urgency, such as an active leak, and includes it in the summary for human review. It is not an emergency response service and does not guarantee response times.

What happens when I request a demo?

The form prepares a draft in your email app addressed to our existing contact. You review and send it yourself. This website does not store or deliver form submissions. Please share business contact information only, without customer or sensitive information.
